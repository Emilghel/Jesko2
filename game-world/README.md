# Lumen Isles

A small explorable low-poly 3D world built with three.js (single HTML file, no build step).

- Procedural island from seeded simplex noise: beaches, meadows, forests, rocky ridges, snowy peaks
- Animated water, drifting clouds, a full day/night cycle with stars, sun and moon
- Third-person explorer with walking, running, jumping and swimming
- 12 glowing shards to find, with a distance and compass hint
- Keyboard + mouse on desktop; virtual joystick, drag-to-look and pinch zoom on touch

Open `index.html` in a browser (it loads three.js r128 from cdnjs). `world.html` is the same page without the outer document tags, used for publishing as a Claude artifact.

Controls: WASD / arrows to walk, Shift to run, Space to jump, drag to look, scroll to zoom. "New island" generates a fresh seed.

# Skyline Penthouse

A first-person walk through a glass penthouse loft above a procedural city.

- Wood-beamed loft with tile floors, a red sofa, palms, a bar table, bookshelf and a balcony with loungers
- Four city moods outside the windows: Crimson Rain (rain, moon, searchlights), Amber Midnight, Golden Bay (sunset, bay, mountains), Neon Snow
- Dark noir-jazz soundtrack synthesized live (walking bass, brushes and ride, Rhodes, muted horn, reverb, vinyl crackle): starts on "Step inside", toggled by the turntable or the Music button
- Switch the lamps on and off
- Desktop: click to look (pointer lock), WASD to walk, E to interact, 1-4 to switch cities. Phone: joystick, drag to look, Use button

Open `penthouse-standalone.html` in a browser. `penthouse.html` is the artifact version.
